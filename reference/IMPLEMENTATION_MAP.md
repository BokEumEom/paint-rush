## V2 changes made from these frames

1. Arena changed from generic colored cubes to cream paper walls, floor grid, doodles, black ink outlines, tall pink/green/purple pillars, low cyan/orange/blue blocks and floating grapple platforms.
2. Enemies changed from two-sphere humanoids to single floating cartoon face blobs with black silhouette outlines, expressive eyes/mouth and small variant horns/spikes.
3. Paint impacts changed from circular decals to irregular blob shapes with satellite droplets and up to ~220 persistent splats.
4. First-person weapon composition changed to left white katana + right yellow/pink/cyan paint gun.
5. Dash/grapple now modifies camera FOV to better match the fast launch sections.
6. HUD moved to reference positions: HP/PAINT top-left, WAVE top-center, score top-right, circular abilities bottom-center.
7. Perk screen rebuilt as three white paper cards with purple/blue/orange headers and pink `PICK A PERK` title.
8. Pause screen rebuilt as a ruled notebook card with pink left margin and yellow resume control.
